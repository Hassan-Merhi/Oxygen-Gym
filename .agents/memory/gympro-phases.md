## Rule
Stop after each phase and wait for user approval before starting the next.

## Phases
1. **Foundation** (DONE) — Login, roles (admin/manager/staff), permissions, sidebar, protected routes
2. **Advanced Settings** (DONE) — Logo upload, receipt customization, currency, backup settings, expanded permissions grid
3. Dashboard KPIs — active members, revenue, expenses, check-ins, expiring soon, low stock, profit
4. Members — CRUD, member fields, check-in, renew, freeze, history
5. Subscription Plans — CRUD, member count per plan, revenue by plan
6. Staff + Payroll — CRUD, payroll generate/mark paid/history/PDF
7. Payments / Daybook — received and paid tracking, ledger
8. Vouchers — cash receipt, payment, expense, customer payment; print/PDF
9. Accounts — cash/bank/sales/expenses/payroll/inventory; ledger; auto-update from all transactions
10. Stock / Inventory — CRUD, purchase tracking, selling, cost permission gating
11. Sales / Barcode — barcode input, cart, complete sale, receipt
12. Backup — daily auto-backup, manual download, history
13. Desktop App Preparation — clean structure for future Electron packaging
