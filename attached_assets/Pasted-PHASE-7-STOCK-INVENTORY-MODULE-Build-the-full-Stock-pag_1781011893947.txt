PHASE 7 – STOCK / INVENTORY MODULE

Build the full Stock page for gym products like water, creatine, protein, etc.

Important:
- Support USD and CDF.
- Store exchange rate used for purchases.
- Staff without View Cost cannot see cost price, profit, stock value, or purchase cost.
- Staff without Manage Inventory cannot add/edit/archive products or stock purchases.
- Selling products will be handled in the Sales phase, but inventory must be ready for it.

1. Products Page

Create /stock page with:
- Product list
- Search
- Filter by category
- Filter by status: Active, Archived, Deleted
- Filter low stock only
- Pagination

Columns:
- Product number: PRD-000001
- Product name
- Barcode
- Category
- Selling price
- Currency
- Quantity in stock
- Low stock alert quantity
- Status
- Actions

If user has View Cost, also show:
- Cost price
- Stock value
- Expected profit per unit

2. Add/Edit Product

Fields:
- Product name
- Product number auto-generated
- Barcode number
- Category
- Supplier
- Cost price
- Selling price
- Currency
- Quantity in stock
- Low stock alert quantity
- Notes
- Status: Active, Archived, Deleted

3. Stock Purchases

Add stock purchase modal:
- Product
- Quantity added
- Cost per unit
- Total cost
- Currency
- Exchange rate used
- Supplier
- Date
- Notes

Rules:
- Increase product quantity
- Update average cost if needed
- Create expense/payment record if marked as paid
- Create cash ledger entry if paid from cash
- Add activity log

4. Inventory Value

Create inventory summary:
- Total products
- Low stock count
- Total quantity
- Total stock value USD
- Total stock value CDF

Hide stock value from users without View Cost.

5. Low Stock

Highlight products where:
quantity <= low stock alert quantity

Add expandable low-stock table.

6. Product History

Each product should have a history view:
- Stock purchases
- Quantity changes
- Future sales deductions placeholder
- Edits
- Archived/restored history

7. Barcode

Allow barcode number to be added now.
Barcode scanning sales will be built in Phase 8.

Prevent duplicate barcode numbers.

8. Permissions

Without page access:
- Cannot open stock page

Without Manage Inventory:
- View only

Without View Cost:
- Hide:
  - Cost price
  - Total cost
  - Stock value
  - Profit per unit
  - Purchase cost

Without View Profit:
- Hide expected profit

9. Activity Logs

Log:
- Product created
- Product edited
- Product archived
- Product restored
- Stock purchase added
- Barcode changed

10. UI Requirements

- Clean table
- Summary cards
- Add product modal
- Add stock purchase modal
- Product details drawer/page
- Loading states
- Empty states
- Toast notifications
- Full EN / FR / AR translations

Stop after Phase 7 and wait for approval.
