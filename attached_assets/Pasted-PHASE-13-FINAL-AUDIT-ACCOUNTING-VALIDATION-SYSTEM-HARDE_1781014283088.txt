SECTION 3 – SALES AUDIT
--------------------------------------------------

Validate all sales.

Check:

- Sale has payment record
- Sale has ledger entry
- Sale has sale items
- Sale total equals item totals
- Sale stock deduction completed

Flag issues.

--------------------------------------------------
SECTION 4 – VOID SALE AUDIT
--------------------------------------------------

Check all voided sales.

Verify:

- Stock restored
- Ledger reversed
- Payment reversed
- Profit reversed

Flag any mismatches.
