Fix accounting only. Do not work on UI/design/other modules except where accounting data is created.

I need the accounting to be correct and consistent across payments, vouchers, sales, members, payroll, stock purchases, cash ledger, and chart of accounts.

Main problems found:

1. Chart of Accounts mapping is weak/wrong

* `chart_of_accounts` exists, but transaction tables store `account` as a text string like `"cash"` or normalized name.
* There is no real `accountId` foreign key.
* `/accounts/chart/:id/statement` only reads from `vouchers`, so payments, sales, payroll, stock purchases, and membership payments do not appear in the account statement.
* Fix: add/use proper `accountId` mapping for accounting transactions, or create a proper accounting entries table with debit/credit rows linked to chart accounts.

2. Payments + vouchers are being mixed incorrectly

* Member enrollment/renewal creates a `payment` and sometimes also creates a `voucher`.
* The cash ledger is written from payment, but account statement is written from voucher.
* This creates split truth: cash ledger sees payments, chart account statement sees vouchers.
* Fix: one accounting source of truth. Either:
  A) every money movement creates a normalized accounting entry, or
  B) cash ledger and account statements both read from the same normalized transaction table.

3. Account statements are incomplete

* Current account statement only shows vouchers.
* Fix account statement to include all accounting movements mapped to that account:

  * membership payments
  * product sales
  * manual vouchers
  * expenses
  * payroll
  * stock purchases paid from cash
  * reversals/cancellations/voids/corrections

4. Sales currency storage is wrong

* In `sales.ts`, if sale currency is CDF, the created payment stores:

  * `amount = totalAmountUsd`
  * `currency = "USD"`
* This loses original sale currency and makes reports confusing.
* Fix: payment should store original sale currency in `amount`/`currency`, and store conversions separately in `amountUsd`/`amountCdf`.

5. Payroll currency storage is wrong

* In payroll pay route, payment stores `amount = netPayUsd`, `currency = "USD"` even if payroll was CDF.
* Fix: store original payroll currency/amount, plus USD/CDF converted fields.

6. Ledger corrections are incomplete

* Payment/voucher update correction only checks amount/direction.
* If currency or exchangeRate changes, ledger is not corrected.
* Fix: correction must trigger when amount, direction, currency, or exchangeRate changes.

7. Ledger writes are not transaction-safe

* `appendLedgerEntry()` reads last balance then inserts.
* This can break under two simultaneous requests.
* Also some ledger entries are written outside the main transaction, like sales.
* Fix: ledger/accounting entries must be written inside the same DB transaction as the source record. Lock or avoid stored running balance; calculate from immutable entries when possible.

8. Cash ledger balance meaning is unclear

* `amountUsd` and `amountCdf` are treated as equivalents, not separate real USD and CDF cash balances.
* If the gym keeps both USD cash and CDF cash separately, then USD transactions should affect USD balance only, and CDF transactions should affect CDF balance only.
* If CDF is only a converted reporting value, rename/report it clearly as equivalent.
* Decide one accounting rule and apply everywhere.

9. Chart account delete is unsafe

* `/accounts/chart/:id` deletes accounts directly.
* It should not delete if transactions exist.
* Prefer soft deactivate `isActive=false`.

10. Audit is not enough for accounting mapping
    Add accounting audit checks:

* completed payments missing accounting entry
* recorded vouchers missing accounting entry
* completed sales missing accounting entry/payment link
* paid payroll missing payment/accounting entry
* stock purchase paid from cash missing accounting entry
* cancelled/voided records missing reversal entry
* accounting entries with invalid/missing accountId
* sum of accounting entries must match cash ledger/report totals

Required fix:
Create a clean accounting posting system.

Preferred implementation:

* Add `accounting_entries` table:

  * id
  * entryDate
  * sourceType
  * sourceId
  * sourceNumber
  * accountId
  * accountNameSnapshot
  * debitUsd
  * creditUsd
  * debitCdf
  * creditCdf
  * currency
  * amount
  * exchangeRate
  * description
  * createdBy
  * createdAt
* Every financial action posts balanced entries.
* Examples:

  * Membership payment: Debit Cash/Bank, Credit Membership Revenue
  * Product sale: Debit Cash, Credit Sales Revenue; optionally Debit COGS, Credit Inventory
  * Expense voucher/payment: Debit Expense, Credit Cash
  * Payroll paid: Debit Payroll Expense, Credit Cash
  * Stock purchase paid cash: Debit Inventory, Credit Cash
  * Void/cancel: post exact reverse entries, never delete history
* Reports and chart account statements must read from `accounting_entries`, not mixed payments/vouchers.

Also add migration/backfill:

* Backfill historical payments, vouchers, sales, payroll, stock purchases into accounting_entries without duplicating.
* Use sourceType + sourceId uniqueness/idempotency.
* For old records where account mapping is missing, default to Cash account and correct revenue/expense account based on category.
* Make this safe to run multiple times.

Acceptance tests:

* Create member payment with selected cash account → appears in cash ledger, accounting entries, and selected account statement exactly once.
* Create manual cash receipt/payment voucher → account statement and cash balance correct.
* Create CDF sale → payment keeps CDF amount and USD/CDF converted fields correct.
* Pay CDF payroll → payment keeps CDF amount and converted fields correct.
* Edit payment amount/currency/rate → old accounting impact reverses and new impact posts.
* Cancel payment/voucher/payroll or void sale → exact reversal posts.
* `/accounts/summary`, `/accounts/profit-loss`, `/accounts/chart/:id/statement`, and `/ledger/balance` agree.
* No double counting between payments and vouchers.
