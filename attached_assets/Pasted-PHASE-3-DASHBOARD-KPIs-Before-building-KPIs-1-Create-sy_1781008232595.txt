PHASE 3 – DASHBOARD KPIs

Before building KPIs:

1. Create system numbering service.

Generate:
- Members: MEM-000001
- Staff: STF-000001
- Plans: PLN-000001
- Payments: PAY-000001
- Vouchers: VCH-000001
- Sales: SAL-000001
- Payroll: PRL-000001

2. Add record status system:
- Active
- Archived
- Deleted

Do not remove soft delete.

-------------------------------------

Now build Dashboard KPI engine.

Create backend services and dashboard cards.

KPI 1: Active Members
Definition:
Members whose status = Active and expiry date >= today.

Card click opens filtered members list.

-------------------------------------

KPI 2: Monthly Subscription Revenue

Calculate:
All membership payments received during current month.

Support:
- USD
- CDF
- Exchange rate conversion

Show:
- Current month
- Previous month
- % change

-------------------------------------

KPI 3: Monthly Expenses

Calculate:
All expenses recorded through vouchers/payments.

Show:
- Current month
- Previous month
- % change

-------------------------------------

KPI 4: Today's Check-ins

Show:
- Total check-ins today
- Hourly trend chart

Card click opens today's attendance.

-------------------------------------

KPI 5: Expiring Soon

Show members expiring:
- 7 days
- 14 days
- 30 days

Card expandable.

Display:
- Name
- Plan
- Expiry date
- Days remaining

-------------------------------------

KPI 6: Low Stock

Show:
Products at or below low-stock threshold.

Expandable table:
- Product
- Current quantity
- Alert quantity

-------------------------------------

KPI 7: Total Profit

Formula:

Total Revenue
minus
Cost Of Goods Sold
minus
Expenses
minus
Payroll

Create centralized profit engine.

Must support:
- Product sales
- Membership sales
- Future services

Do not hardcode formulas in dashboard.

-------------------------------------

Additional Dashboard Widgets

1. Revenue chart
- Last 12 months

2. Expense chart
- Last 12 months

3. Membership growth chart

4. Recent activity feed
Use activity_logs table.

-------------------------------------

Permission rules:

Staff without:
- View Cost
- View Profit

Cannot see:
- Profit cards
- Profit charts
- Cost information

Display "Access Restricted".

-------------------------------------

Dashboard must load from real database queries.
No mock data.
No hardcoded values.

After Phase 3 is complete, stop and wait for approval.
